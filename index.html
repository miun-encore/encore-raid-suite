How to Read These Charts

Healing is a decision-making game, so timing and attention are the real resources we all manage. This chart classifies healers by how their attention spreads and how active they were doing so.

X axis shows casts per minute, but normalized by global cooldowns (nCPM). A 3-second cast counts as 2 GCDs, a 1.5s Flash Heal counts as 1. This removes bias for slower casting playstyles.

Y axis shows the participation ratio, a measure of how many targets someone was actively healing (Neff). If a druid casts the same amount of spells on all forty players, N equals 40. If a paladin sticks to a tank 1000 times but spot-heals once or twice on other players, N equals 1.

Healers on the right are more active. Healers on the top are whack-a-moling across the raid.

💡 A good rule of thumb for a healthy Neff is: number of direct assignments plus 5.
